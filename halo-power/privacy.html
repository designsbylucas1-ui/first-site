Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Halo Power Solutions Inc. ("Halo", "we") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us in the quote form: your name, phone number, email, the service you want, your project details, and any photos you choose to attach.
  • Anything you send us by phone, email or text.

We do not run advertising trackers on this website. This site does not use cookies for tracking.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To schedule and carry out work you ask us to do.
  • To keep records of our work, as required for our business.

We do not sell your information.

Who handles it

Quote form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, a form service, and reach us by email. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. Our website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. These services handle data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and job records for as long as needed to serve you and meet business and tax obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to us contacting you, at any time. Contact us at hello@halopower.ca or (780) 995-0359.
