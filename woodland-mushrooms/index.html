Fresh & culinary

A little forest on your plate

We grow a variety of specialty mushrooms for culinary purposes. King oyster, pioppino, lion's mane, chestnut, blue oyster, elm oyster and shiitake are some of the more common strains we grow. They fruit on hardwoods that we source organically and locally.

Medicinal

Cordyceps Militaris

A medicinal mushroom with thousands of years of use on its resume. Known for immune support, heart and lung health, anti-inflammatory properties, and a natural boost in energy.

We take great pride in growing our cordyceps on organic, plant-based, food-grade materials, with close attention to the nutrition and environment that let them thrive.
