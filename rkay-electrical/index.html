About

Every home is different. The care we put in isn't.

Rkay Electrical is a residential electrical contractor focused on new home construction. Some homes get a Control4 hardwired-switch system with a lot of automation, others a Crestron panelized-lighting setup, our standard Levven Electronics package, or a straightforward wired system.

The one thing they all have in common is the care and detail our installers put into making every panel as neat and orderly as possible.

Services

What we do

New home wiring

Full rough-in and finish for builders, with tidy, code-compliant work from panel to plate.

Panels & distribution

Clean, labelled panels and structured wiring that are easy to service for years to come.

Lighting design installs

Pot lights, feature pendants, coves and under-cabinet LEDs that make a space feel finished.

Smart-home & automation

Control4, Crestron and Levven systems, wired in properly from the start.

Renovations

Upgrades and rework for existing homes, including downtown penthouse projects.

Responsible clean-up

We collect copper scrap from every site and take it to the copper depot, keeping it out of the landfill.

Sustainability

Copper stays out of the landfill

At Rkay we collect the vast majority of our copper wiring scraps from every site during clean-up and bring them to the copper depot. Less waste, tidier job sites.
