FAQ

Questions people ask.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted as a flat price after our first call, so there are no surprise invoices. Tell me what you need and I'll send a number.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I handle the build, hosting setup and launch. You just tell me about your business.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Most customers look on their phone, so every site is designed for phones first.

Who owns the site?

You do. If you ever want to move on, everything goes with you.

Do you work outside Edmonton?

Mostly Sherwood Park and Edmonton, but calls and email work from anywhere, so ask.
